How a story earns its place

  1. Scan. Every headline from Nepal's major outlets in the last 24 hours, in Nepali and English.
  2. Debate. An advocate and a skeptic research each story and argue it out.
  3. Rank. Two judges in sequence decide what runs today.
  4. Write. A writer files the story from the original reports, with every number sourced.
  5. Check. A red team attacks accuracy, relevance, defensibility and reach. A defence answers. Two more judges rule.
  6. Publish. Only stories approved by both final judges appear here, with their full review record.
